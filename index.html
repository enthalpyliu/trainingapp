Safety notes

  • This tracker is not a diagnostic tool or substitute for an individualized assessment.
  • Because you have had recurrent episodes of significant low-back pain, arrange a non-urgent assessment with a physical therapist or clinician before progressing to heavy lifting.
  • Seek urgent medical care for new bladder/bowel dysfunction, saddle numbness, or progressive leg weakness.
  • Do not train through sharp, increasing, radiating, or neurologic symptoms.
